# Harper — Apache License 2.0

https://github.com/Automattic/harper

BetterTeX's English grammar checker (`Sources/BetterTeXCore/GrammarChecker.swift`) adapts Harper's LaTeX masking,
indefinite-article and grammar rules to Swift, and `GrammarPhrases.json` is generated from Harper's
`harper-core/src/linting/phrase_set_corrections/mod.rs` (commit b5cd1a2). These parts were modified for BetterTeX:
they were rewritten in Swift, some rules were simplified, and programming-specific phrase rules were left out.
